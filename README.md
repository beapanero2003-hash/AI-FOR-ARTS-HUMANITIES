## INTRODUCTION
This AI for Arts & Humanities portfolio gathers a compilation of practical activities (Parts 2 to 4) developed throughout the course following the guidelines of the Course Lab Book. The exercises found within the repository do not cover the entirety of the weekly lab practice, but rather aim to exhibit the main tasks that lays the foundation for the progression followed throughout the course. Each exercise covers the context, a step-by-step account along with a critical examination of the aforementioned. As a result, this portfolio will not only focus on the technical dimension embedded within the activities, but will also explore the line of reasoning. 
To start with, the main aim of this portfolio is to showcase how coding, algorithms and machine learning are attainable processes for Art and Humanities backgrounds. In addition to this, a particular interest lies in establishing a conection between the technical and the cultural pespective which deals with data management and the ethical concerns raised by the emergence of AI.
